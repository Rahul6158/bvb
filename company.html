2. "What exactly do you do at Gamyam?"

Answer:
I'm mainly responsible for the engineering side of data extraction.

That includes understanding how the source exposes its data, building the extraction logic, handling APIs and browser-based extraction when required, processing and validating the results, and integrating everything with our backend systems.

I've also worked on the infrastructure side with AWS, SQS, databases, Redis and monitoring.

3. "What is the biggest project you've worked on there?"

Answer (Shopee Multi-Market Pipeline):
One of the bigger projects I've worked on was a multi-market Shopee extraction pipeline.

It was designed as an asynchronous system where clients submit jobs and receive a job ID. The jobs are then distributed to different workers depending on the extraction approach.

The workers extract the data, return the results for processing, and successful records are validated and stored. Failed jobs can be retried, and the overall pipeline is monitored using Grafana and alerts.

The interesting part for me was that it wasn't just about scraping a page — it involved designing the complete distributed processing workflow.

At this point, I'm looking for an environment where I can work on more challenging problems, learn from highly experienced engineers and deepen my understanding of large-scale data and engineering systems.

That's what attracted me to this opportunity at DE Shaw.

Rule: Keep it 100% positive. Don't say anything negative about Gamyam, management, or workload.

5. "What did you learn at Gamyam?"

Answer (Scraper vs. Production System):
The biggest thing I learned is that building a scraper and building a reliable data-extraction system are very different things.

Initially, the focus can be on simply getting the data. But when you operate at scale, you have to think about retries, concurrency, duplicate processing, data quality, monitoring, database performance and failure recovery.

Working on these systems gave me a much better understanding of production engineering.

Why this works: Connects your Gamyam experience directly to why you are ready for DE Shaw's Data Extraction Engineer role.
